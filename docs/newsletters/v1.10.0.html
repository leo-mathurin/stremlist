Filters and search for your watchlists

Hi there, a long watchlist is great until you have to pick something. This update gives you a few more ways to find what fits tonight.

Filter down to what you feel like watching

Choose a genre, decade, maximum runtime, or minimum IMDb rating for each catalog. Combine them for something specific, like a drama lasting 90 minutes or less, rated 7 or above.

Search your own catalogs

Find titles from your lists through Stremio search. Saved filters apply to the results too, and accents won't get in the way. You can also try a different filter or sort from the catalog dropdown without changing your saved settings.

Three extra catalogs for your home

Add "90 min or less", "Top rated", or "Shuffle" views of the same list to your Stremio home. They use your saved filters, so you can keep a catalog of shorter movies or a shuffled selection ready to browse.

Since the last newsletter, support has also grown to 15,000 titles per IMDb watchlist or list, and catalogs start preparing after you save. This release fixes TV episode selection when Stremlist comes before other metadata addons. Keep another metadata provider installed for series details.

Open Configure and expand "Filters & extra catalogs" on a list. Save your choices, then reinstall the addon in Stremio to pick up the new catalogs, search, and dropdown options.
